# Portraits & skin rendering

Recipe choice for faces is mostly about **how the recipe treats skin texture and tone**. The deciding variable is usually the *subject's skin*, not the light.

## Principles

- **Clarity & Sharpness are the skin dial.** High Clarity/Sharpness renders pores, lines, and texture. On a **characterful / aged / adult-male face** that texture reads as honest character — it flatters. On **smooth-skin / beauty** subjects it exaggerates flaws — use a soft recipe (low Clarity, Sharpness ≤ 0).
- **EV is the second skin dial.** With a high-Clarity recipe, *brighter* exposure softens skin and de-emphasizes texture; *slightly darker* models the face with more character. Tune flattery with exposure before changing recipe.
- **Match the recipe to the face, then the light.** Same lighting, different subject → different recipe.
- **Tone sets the register.** Warm toned mono (Sepia) reads timeless/heirloom — strong for elder portraits. Soft warm color (Astia/[[recipes/soft-color-universe]]) flatters general skin. Avoid Velvia/high-contrast on faces.
- **Soft directional window light** is a reliable, flattering portrait key — wraps the face, gentle falloff.
- **Environmental portraits:** the background should *say something* (subject's space), not just fill the frame. Same subtraction discipline as [[concepts/composition-and-subtraction]] — busy ≠ contextual.

## Recipe map (so far)

- **Characterful / aged / adult-male face, mono** → [[recipes/brians-sepia]] (texture = character; timeless tone; EV tunes softness).
- **General skin, color** → [[recipes/soft-color-universe]] (warm, soft, flattering).
- **Cool/muted skin, color** → [[recipes/pastel-vibes]] (deprioritized by taste).
- **Avoid on faces:** [[recipes/bay-area-fortia]] (Velvia over-saturates skin), high-contrast tonal recipes ([[recipes/loki]]) unless a deliberate gritty character study.
