## Key claims (each citable)

- **Black-and-white only**, categorically — refuses color even hypothetically.
- **Night is a core love**: atmosphere/mood, and night delivers high contrast *"without having to work very hard for it"* — the ambient light does the work.
- **Night kit**: Leica M10 Monochrom; fast primes — 24mm Summilux f/1.4 + a 50mm f/1.4. *"A 1.4 lens can gather twice as much light as an F2 lens."*
- **Settings**: aperture priority; **Auto ISO capped at 6400**; shutter floor **1/125 s**; **exposure compensation ≈ −2**, especially shooting into halogens.
- **Underexpose rationale (authoritative)**: *"most of the dynamic range comes from boosting out of the shadows rather than cutting back on highlight, so it's the smart move to underexpose on digital."* Requires RAW + the ability to **boost in post**.
- *"You don't need much light, you just need a little bit to create some magic."*
- **Wet pavement after rain at night → a "lovely burst of highlights"**; the wet-down "Sheen" look is prized.
- **Composition**: wait for the subject to be *under* a light; backlight + heavy negative space; uses the camera's blinkies to place **pure-black and pure-white deliberately next to the subject's head**; 24mm vanishing-point "corridor."
- One favorite frame shot at **ISO 20,000** (a mistake) "held because it's the Monochrom" — a *dedicated mono sensor* tolerance.
- **Night = fewer people** → isolation/quiet, which he favors (single subjects, couples, small groups).
- **Process**: patience, planning, pre-visualize the ideal frame, set up and wait; *ears* guide you (no music while shooting).
- **Light is "the glue"** — a subject of its own; bad light = a missing essential.
- Best night cities: London, Tokyo, Hong Kong, Paris, Havana.
