# Reference matrix — 7 recipes, one scene

All 7 custom recipes shot from the **same campus vantage** (Wesley Foundation steps / tower, dusk→night), every variable held constant except the recipe. Completed 2026-05-17. This is the controlled experiment that makes the differences objective rather than impressionistic. Full per-shoot detail in [[synthesis/debriefs]].

## The scene, by recipe

| Recipe | Sim | Rendering of the identical scene |
|---|---|---|
| [[recipes/soft-color-universe]] | Astia | Richest believable color — deep cobalt sky, warm pop. The **default color** benchmark. |
| [[recipes/pastel-vibes]] | Classic Neg | Cooler, muted, flatter; restrained. Needs scene structure or goes limp. |
| [[recipes/bay-area-fortia]] | Velvia | Most saturated sky of all — but least natural. Max pop, realism traded. |
| [[recipes/loki]] | Classic Neg | Warm, dark, crushed, near-mono. *Erases* the blue — assertive/inky. |
| [[recipes/junichiro]] | PRO Neg. Hi | Soft, cool, faded, grainy. Erases the blue too — wistful, gentler on lamps. |
| [[recipes/brians-sepia]] | Sepia | Clean warm toned-mono (no grain). Versatile **default mono**, modern not vintage. |
| [[recipes/schaller]] | ACROS+R | Neutral, dramatic deep-black B&W, subtle grain. Fine-art geometry/negative-space. |

## Axes the matrix revealed

- **Color saturation/realism dial:** Junichiro/Loki (erased) → Pastel Vibes (muted) → SCU (rich, believable) → Bay Area Fortia (max, processed).
- **Two defaults:** SCU = versatile color default; Brian's Sepia = versatile mono default. Both "work in most lighting" (Sepia: *when lit*).
- **Tonal cluster (color that behaves like B&W):** Loki (assertive) vs Junichiro (wistful) — pick by mood.
- **Mono pair:** Brian's Sepia (warm, clean, modern, gentler) vs Schaller (neutral, dramatic, fine-art, geometric).
- **Film sim sets blue-hour sky impact**; **DR/Highlight sets night-lamp control** (Junichiro/Sepia/Fortia DR200 > Loki DR100).
- **When night artificial color is problematic → go mono** (Schaller fixed what Fortia clipped on the same McKnight facade).
