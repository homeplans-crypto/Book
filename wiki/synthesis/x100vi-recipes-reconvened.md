# X100VI Recipes Reconvened — The Reading, the Verdicts, the Revised Kit

Reconvened panel consensus (2026-07-03), superseding the recipe choices of [[synthesis/x100vi-oklahoma-recipes]]. The panel re-judged everything against [[sources/brian-photographic-preferences]] and the corrected [[sources/brian-style-statement]] ("believable realism, streets as landscapes"), which arrived mid-panel and was applied as the authoritative brief.

Panel: 4 reread analysts/scouts + 4 lineage experts (tonalist, Berrada-school, plains-documentary, night specialist; a fifth expert and the first critic wave were lost to a session limit and re-run), then 5 critics (realism, streets-as-landscape, Fuji-technical, coherence, SOOC-Oklahoma) and a neutral chair. The panel deliberately did NOT pad to seven recipes — slots are artistic voices, not coverage.

## The reading — what the recipes say about Brian

The recipes say Brian is two photographers sharing one camera, and the corrected brief is the moment he chose which one leads. The BANK says: a contained-light material poet — four of seven slots are Classic Negative, two of them crushed (C2 H0/S+4, C6 Loki inherited from Berrada unchanged), one self-described as 'imposing a warm cinematic world' (C4 Honō). That is a stylized, shadow-worshipping, Tanizaki-lineage kit, and the reconvened experts read it faithfully — which is why they over-produced tonalist builds (Nostalgic Neg fade, Natura grain-mush, a 7500K char-world) that the corrected brief now bans by name. The INVARIANTS say something different: NR −4 universal, corpus-validated parameters over vibes, a day/night curve principle derived from sensor mechanics, and — decisively — his documented night-street practice: Reala Ace, Daylight WB for source separation, DR400. That practice IS the corrected brief ('streets as landscapes,' lamps controlled, local color held) executed before it was written. The reconvene's best proposals are not discoveries; they are Brian's own existing habit formalized, which is why four experts converged on them independently. The honest tension: what he SHOOTS at close range (weathered material, crushed wine-red shadow, lush Loki low-key) is obviously stylized by his own corrected definition, while what he now SAYS he wants for streets and places is gently-shaped realism. The reconciliation is domain, not conversion: the crushed-CN voices are legitimate for intimate material still life — a flower in shade is not a place — but the moment the subject is the ENVIRONMENT (street, facade, curb, lamp, sky), his standard flips to believability, and the brief's 'slightly poetic' is carried by light, hour, and weather, not by grade. The one slot the corrected brief puts under real pressure is C4 Honō: 'imposes a warm cinematic world' and 'the image should feel like being there' cannot both be true of the same weather photograph. The experts were too deferential to name it; this panel names it and puts the question to Brian rather than auto-fixing. Last, unsentimentally: the tonalist self-description ('quiet, grounded, textured, found') survives the correction intact — nothing in the realism brief contradicts 'found'; what dies is the idea that texture comes from emulsion simulation. On a 40MP sensor at NR −4, Brian's texture is pavement, brick, and faded paint, resolved — grain and negative sharpness were always someone else's costume.

## Disposition of the first panel's seven

### Gaillardia (Astia flower close-up) — **KEEP**

Untouched by the course-correction: it was already realist craft (Clarity 0, Grain Off, honest Astia, CCE Strong as gradation engineering, not a look). But it is a flower recipe on a brief about streets-as-environment, and Brian's own C5 Pastel Bloom owns flora — so it keeps bench status for planned flower sessions rather than contending for a place-slot.

*Surviving lesson:* CCE Strong as the load-bearing gradation-protection tool inside saturated red/yellow; R-channel blinkies because red clips before the luminance meter warns; Clarity 0 as wind-tempo discipline.

### Kodachrome 64 (X-Trans V Oklahoma tune) — **RETIRE**

The +2R/−3B permanent warm shift and postcard-Americana register are 'warmth usually subtle' violated by default; it was the wrong-brief workhorse and its territory is fully covered by Setback (masonry) and Vernacular Ace (mixed street) with believable color.

*Surviving lesson:* CCFX Blue Off is the verified X-Trans V Classic Chrome porting rule; CCE Strong holds hematite red-orange; EC capped at 0/−1/3 in Oklahoma high sun (FXW's +guidance is calibrated for softer light).

### Prairie Ace (Reala Ace baseline) — **REVISE**

The closest the old kit came to the corrected brief; it becomes Vernacular Ace directly. Revisions: Color +3→+2 (vivid-brief residue), Clarity +2→0 (SOOC tempo, no write pause), Sharpness +1 kept, WB re-scoped by venue instead of the manual 6pm golden-hour flip, red-dirt mode defanged (+1R deleted).

*Surviving lesson:* Mode-based recipe design (documented red-dirt / golden-hour / glass modes on one chassis) is the correct slot-economy pattern and survives into the new kit wholesale.

### Red Dirt Big Sky (disciplined Velvia) — **RETIRE**

'Overcooked saturation' is excluded by Brian's corrected words per se; even disciplined Velvia is a maximum-punch slide look, i.e., an imposed register. Big-sky-as-picture is also subject-chasing the brief demotes, and C4/C1 hold weather and rural distance.

*Surviving lesson:* Color +2 + CCE Strong as anti-clip architecture beats Color +4 — the general principle that saturation-gradation work belongs to CCE, not the Color slider.

### Deco Chrome (Classic Chrome, de-faded) — **REVISE**

The DR100-at-base-ISO skeleton (true blacks, zero curve manipulation) is the most physically honest hard-sun rendering in either kit and squarely on-brief. Its sins were finish, not structure: Clarity +3's write pause and halo risk. The reconvened experts revised it in the wrong direction (Grain Strong, negative Sharpness — the mirror doctrine); the correct revision is the realist Setback: Clarity 0, Sharpness 0/+1, Grain Off, Color 0, Shadow +1.5.

*Surviving lesson:* DR100@ISO125 = mechanically honest deep blacks with a MANDATORY DR200/250 escape near glass or cumulus; WB Daylight +1R/0B counters Classic Chrome's teal drift on limestone/terracotta; RAW+JPEG for the free Acros+R reprocess.

### Cumulus Polarizer (Provia big-sky) — **RETIRE**

Retirement stands, but the chair strikes the reconvened experts' rationale before it is filed: they executed it for anti-tonalist crimes ('neutrality is the enemy, atmosphere is the subject') — reasoning the corrected brief overrules, since believable realism partially rehabilitates exactly this neutral register. The valid grounds are redundancy and subject: it is a sky recipe, not a street recipe; C4 owns weather and Vernacular Ace is the better honest daylight base.

*Surviving lesson:* H−1.5 as the cumulus/highlight-discipline reference number; the first-frame CCFX-Strong zenith verify as a standing habit; its DR/ISO-floor debugging as index lint rules.

### Curtain Wall Ace (honest OKC glass) — **REVISE**

CHAIR OVERRULES the reconvened experts' unanimous demote-to-lesson: they dismissed daytime OKC glass on 'anti-wabi-sabi' grounds the corrected brief explicitly reverses — OKC glass is in Brian's environment list, and this recipe's H−2 + DR400 + Daylight lock is the single best 'controlled highlights' engineering in either kit; it was never stylized (Grain Off, Sharp +1, honest Reala Ace). Revision: Clarity +2→0; then the chassis serves twice — as Vernacular Ace's documented 'hard glass' day mode and as the skeleton of After Dark Ace — so it costs no slot while daytime curtain walls stay covered SOOC.

*Surviving lesson:* The H−2 + DR400 specular stack is the maximum in-JPEG highlight protection and the kit's answer to 'artificial lights controlled, not blown out'; WB Daylight 0R/−1B locked because AWB is reliably fooled by frame-filling blue reflection; EC metered on the brightest glazed panel.

## Method

Chair read the two authoritative documents (raw/brian-photographic-preferences.md; wiki/synthesis/x100vi-oklahoma-recipes.md) via the Read tool. The cached panel-work path was passed as 'undefined' and no panel files exist in the repo (verified by glob), so phase-2 evidence was taken from the five critic reviews supplied in full in the chair's brief, each of which quotes the reconvened experts' proposals, settings blocks, and old-kit verdicts verbatim. Consensus procedure: (1) collapse the experts' thirteen proposals to five distinct builds per the coherence critic's arithmetic; (2) score each build against the corrected realism brief using critic convergence — a build survives only where at least four of five critics rate it a keeper (Vernacular Ace 8/8/9/9/8; After Dark Ace/House Hunting 8.5/8.5/9+8/9/9+8.5; Setback 6.5/7/7/8/6.5-with-fixes) and dies where the critics converge on 'stylized' (Nostalgic Neg pair 3–4/10 across all five; Natura pair 3–5/10; Yakisugi 3.5–4/10); (3) arbitrate every unreconciled parameter fork by explicit critic-majority vote with physics tie-breaks (night Color +1 over +2 on HPS legibility; Sharpness 0/+1 over the experts' negative values on the brief's surface-detail clause; Grain Off over Weak/Strong on 40MP resolution grounds); (4) enforce Brian's hard invariants as gates, not preferences — NR −4 universal, SOOC-final Clarity 0, day/night curve principle with the two argued exceptions flagged for sign-off, and the corpus-validation rule (which disqualified Yakisugi's unverified Color outright); (5) overturn exactly two of the experts' old-kit verdicts where the corrected brief reverses their premise (Curtain Wall Ace demote → revise/absorb; Cumulus Polarizer rationale rewritten from anti-atmosphere to redundancy before filing); (6) add the four contracts every critic flagged as missing — blue-hour metering, night legibility acceptance test, LED-corridor WB mode, dusk hand-off trigger.
