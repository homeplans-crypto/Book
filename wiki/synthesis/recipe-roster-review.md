# Recipe roster review

Living scorecard for deciding which of the 7 [[entities/x100vi]] custom slots to **keep, update, or swap**, optimized for the [[synthesis/photographer-profile|photographer's actual practice]] (outdoor nature / quiet streets, mostly night & sunrise, light-on-objects-at-night, people rarely, vacations need reliable color). Re-run via the **Roster review** workflow in `CLAUDE.md`. Re-edited each review (not append-only).

**Hard guardrail:** this page only *proposes*. No camera slot changes until the proposed UPDATE/SWAP is validated by a controlled A/B vs. the incumbent and filed as a `debrief`.

## Rubric (score 1–5, must trace to a citable wiki line)

1. **Practice alignment** — serves the owner's real conditions/subjects?
2. **Earned evidence** — experience-confirmed, distinct strength?
3. **Distinctiveness** — unique niche vs. overlaps a sibling?
4. **Reliability** — portable, or condition-dependent with a precondition that *actually occurs* in practice?
5. **Aesthetic fit** — owner actually likes & reaches for it?

## Dispositions

- **Slot 1 SCU — KEEP.** Primary/vacation, portable, cross-test confirmed. Only open: high-ISO night NR−4 (UPDATE-watch, not blocking).
- **Slot 3 Loki — KEEP.** Purpose-built for the signature subject (light-on-objects-at-night); that subject supplies its hard-light + dark precondition, so its condition-dependence is moot here.
- **Slot 6 Brian's Sepia — KEEP.** Versatile mono default; portable when lit.
- **Slot 7 Schaller — KEEP.** Only fine-art neutral geometry/architecture B&W; night-capable; distinct from Sepia.
- **Slot 4 Junichiro — KEEP (watch).** Distinct wistful mood vs Loki + better night-lamp control; night-aligned. But it twins Loki's Berrada/EV−2/3 family — it is the **#2 swap candidate** if a slot is later needed.
- **Slot 5 Bay Area Fortia — KEEP (re-evaluate).** Confirmed real gray/rain-rescue capability + vacation utility, but daytime-aligned vs. a night/sunrise practice. Retention is **evidence-tied**: track in debriefs how often gray/rain actually occurs; if rare across a season, becomes a swap candidate.
- **Slot 2 Pastel Vibes — SWAP.** Redundant with the SCU soft/Color+3 family (cooler/muted Classic Neg vs warmer Astia), over-softens in flat light, and **taste-deprioritized** by the photographer. Lowest marginal value of the 7. Free this slot.

## Swap-candidate backlog (priority order)

1. **Sunrise-tuned color recipe** — core practice, currently uncovered/untested (open in [[synthesis/photographer-profile]]). Highest priority.
2. **Classic Chrome street/travel look** — unrepresented film sim; understated, fits quiet city streets + travel; complements SCU without duplicating it.
3. **Light-on-objects-at-night refinement** — only if Loki shows gaps in future debriefs. Low priority.

## Queued A/B (gates the slot-2 swap)

Before Pastel Vibes leaves slot 2:
1. Build the top backlog candidate (start: sunrise color recipe; fallback: Classic Chrome).
2. Controlled comparison, same method as [[synthesis/reference-matrix]] / the fern single-subject matrix: shoot **candidate + Pastel Vibes + SCU (control)** on one scene in the target condition (sunrise; or a quiet street / travel-typical scene for Classic Chrome).
3. Decision gate: candidate must clearly beat Pastel Vibes for a practice-relevant condition **and** not duplicate SCU or Brian's Sepia.
4. File as a `debrief`. Only then write the candidate into slot 2 and mark Pastel Vibes retired (keep its recipe page with a dated "retired from camera slot 2" note — the wiki keeps history).
