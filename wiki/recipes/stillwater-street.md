A muted, structured [[entities/classic-chrome]] street/travel look — the **taste-aligned replacement for [[recipes/bay-area-fortia]]'s gray-rescue role**. Where Fortia lifts flat light with raw Velvia saturation (and over-eggs the orange-heavy local environment), this lifts it with **contrast and character**: Classic Chrome's restrained palette + Color Chrome Strong / FX Blue Strong for depth, Highlight +1 / Shadow +2 / Clarity +2 for structure, WB shift R−1 B+1 for a cool documentary cast that resists the local orange. Reach for it in overcast, rain, quiet streets, and travel where you want the gray fought without warm-color amplification. Caveats: Adobe RGB (export sRGB for web); JPEG; DR200 needs ISO ≥ ~320.
