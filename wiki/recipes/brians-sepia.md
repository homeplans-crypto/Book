The wiki's first toned-monochrome recipe — [[entities/sepia]] pushed hard: Highlight +2 / Shadow +3 for deep, contrasty tones; Sharpness +3 and Clarity +3 for crisp, gritty texture. (Color is n/a — Sepia has no saturation control; WB R+3 B−5 still nudges the warm tone.) Reach for it in strong directional light, textured subjects, and graphic street/architecture where tone and form carry the image. Avoid flat overcast — the high tone curve needs real shadows to work. Caveat: Adobe RGB, JPEG.
