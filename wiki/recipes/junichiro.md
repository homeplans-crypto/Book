A dark, faded, low-saturation look by [[entities/mehdi-berrada]] on [[entities/pro-neg-hi]]. Color −4 strips it nearly to muted monochrome-adjacent tones; Sharpness −2 plus Strong/Small grain gives a soft analog texture; Shadow +1 deepens blacks while Highlight 0 keeps a filmic roll-off; WB R+2 B−5 warms it slightly. The author shoots it at EV −2/3 and composes for "black negative space and patterns of light." Reach for it in moody, low-key, high-shadow scenes where the subject is light against dark — not for bright, evenly-lit, colorful scenes (Color −4 leaves them lifeless). Caveat: Adobe RGB, JPEG.

> Source note: designed for the [[entities/x100vi]]'s predecessor (X100V / X-Trans 4). Transfers, but verify grain rendering and PRO Neg. Hi tonality on X-Trans 5 first shoot.

> Self note 2026-05-17: not yet shot — debrief after first use; confirm grain Strong/Small isn't overdone on the 40MP sensor.

Companion to [[recipes/loki]] — both Berrada, both EV −2/3 deep/dark looks. Split: Loki is high-contrast and graphic (Classic Neg); Junichiro is soft, grainy, desaturated (PRO Neg. Hi).
