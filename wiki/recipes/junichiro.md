A dark, faded, low-saturation look by [[entities/mehdi-berrada]] on [[entities/pro-neg-hi]]. Color −4 strips it nearly to muted monochrome-adjacent tones; Sharpness −2 plus Strong/Small grain gives a soft analog texture; Shadow +1 deepens blacks while Highlight 0 keeps a filmic roll-off; WB R+2 B−5 warms it slightly. The author shoots it at EV −2/3 and composes for "black negative space and patterns of light." Reach for it in moody, low-key, high-shadow scenes where the subject is light against dark — not for bright, evenly-lit, colorful scenes (Color −4 leaves them lifeless). Caveat: Adobe RGB, JPEG.

> Source note (resolved 2026-05-17, [[synthesis/debriefs]]): **transfers cleanly to the [[entities/x100vi]] / X-Trans 5** — soft/faded/desaturated look intact, and **Grain Strong/Small is fine on the 40MP sensor at low ISO** (fine, filmic, not overdone). This was the last open Berrada X-Trans 5 question; closed.

> Self note 2026-05-17 (shot, [[synthesis/debriefs]]): same-scene + same-subject A/B vs [[recipes/loki]]. Junichiro = **soft, cool-neutral, faded, grainy, melancholic**; Loki = hard, warm, crushed, crisp, dramatic. Same family, opposite emotional register. Junichiro also **controls night point sources better** (DR200 + Highlight 0 vs Loki's DR100 + Highlight +4 — less lamp blowout). Performed well at blue hour *and* on directional-lit grass → safer/more versatile within its desaturated register; `best_for` widened (texture, soft-light).

Companion to [[recipes/loki]] — both Berrada, both EV −2/3 deep/dark looks. Split: Loki is high-contrast and graphic (Classic Neg); Junichiro is soft, grainy, desaturated (PRO Neg. Hi).
