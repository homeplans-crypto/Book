A muted-rich **cinematic** look on [[entities/eterna|Eterna/Cinema]] — slot **CUSTOM 3**, the gray/overcast/rain quiet-street tool (replaced [[recipes/stillwater-street]] there 2026-05-18). Eterna's low-contrast, low-saturation film base is pushed back up the *other* way from a saturation recipe: **Color +4** restores richness *within* Eterna's restraint (so it reads deep, not garish), **Clarity +5** (max) + Sharpness +2 manufacture the microstructure flat/wet light strips out, Shadow +1.5 / Highlight 0 add gentle body without crushing. Neutral WB (Auto White Priority, no shift). Caveats: Monochromatic Color n/a (color sim); Adobe RGB (export sRGB for web); Smooth Skin off; JPEG; DR200 needs ISO ≥ ~320. **Watch:** Clarity +5 is the ceiling — risk of over-crunch/halos at higher ISO; Color +4 on Eterna is a big lift — fine because the base is flat, but watch already-colorful scenes (milder analog of the Fortia over-egg rule).

> Self note 2026-05-18 (intent — first-class, [[synthesis/photographer-profile]]): owner's stated intent for this slot is **"a distinctively different look from anything else, within the palette."** Eterna was the choice because it delivers that aesthetic distinctness *while staying taste-aligned* (no warm push, no grain, no nostalgia). **The role is therefore aesthetic-distinctness, not condition-bound** — gray/rain/indoor/orange-safe is *earned evidence* (where it has been shot), not the recipe's stated remit. Pick it whenever a deliberately different cinematic register is wanted, in any condition; not narrowly "the gray slot."

> Self note 2026-05-18 (first field evidence — rain, [[synthesis/debriefs]]): two frames in **active rain** at the same property as the 2026-05-18 Stillwater-Street A/B (5188/5189). **Handles the OSU-orange brick taste-appropriately** — Eterna's desaturated base + neutral WB tame the brick (muted, not amplified), so the slot-3 role (gray/rain, no warm over-egg) is **still covered, by a different mechanism** than Stillwater Street's cool-documentary palette. Wet pavement renders well (somber cinematic palette + crisp Clarity suits rain — Schaller's "no bad light / wet = mood"). This is the first **active-rain** evidence in the wiki and re-answers A/B #2's slot-3 question for the *new* occupant.

> Self note 2026-05-18 (recipe characterization — indoor objects, [[synthesis/debriefs]]): owner shot a resin figurine + a Blue Yeti mic *to test rendering, not composition.* Curtain Call is **more than a weather tool — a strong indoor detailed-object / still-life renderer**: Clarity +5 makes texture (carved fur, foam, knit) crisp and dimensional; Color +4 on Eterna keeps pastels/colours rich-not-garish; Eterna + Shadow +1.5 holds dark subjects open with controlled metallic highlights; blurred warm/orange backgrounds stay tamed (orange-safe indoors too). `best_for` widened (still-life, indoor, detailed-object, texture). **Caution:** the same Clarity +5 that flatters texture will *harshen smooth/young skin* — not a people recipe (mirror of the [[concepts/portraits-and-skin-rendering]] texture rule).

Distinct from [[recipes/stillwater-street]] (retired from CUSTOM 3): both lift flat/gray light without warm over-egg, but Stillwater Street = Classic Chrome cool-documentary structure; Curtain Call = Eterna cinematic-muted richness + max microcontrast.
