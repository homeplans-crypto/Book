A soft, saturated-but-gentle color look. [[entities/astia]] base with both tone curves pulled down (Highlight −1 / Shadow −1) for low contrast and protected highlights, while Color +3 and Strong Color Chrome push rich, smooth saturation; Clarity +3 adds midtone definition so the image doesn't go flat. Reach for it in soft or directional daylight, golden hour, and relaxed portraits where you want pleasant skin and pastel-leaning color rather than punch. Caveats: shot in **Adobe RGB** color space (convert/export to sRGB for web) with Smooth Skin off; JPEG recipe, DR200 needs ISO ≥ ~320 to engage.

> Self note 2026-05-17 (first shoot, [[synthesis/debriefs]]): excelled at **blue hour / mixed artificial+ambient light** — rich cobalt sky vs. amber lamplight, clean warm/cool separation, Auto WB held the mood, DR200 + Highlight −1 protected the lit facade. Performed well *outside* its original daylight/portrait `best_for`; `blue-hour` added. Limit unfound: NR −4 at high ISO untested. See [[concepts/blue-hour-mixed-light]].

> Self note 2026-05-17 (cross-test): across wider field testing this recipe **holds up in most lighting**, not just its strongest conditions. Treat it as the **versatile default / safe everyday color recipe** — reach for the specialized recipes when you want a specific *look*, not because Soft Color Universe fails. `versatile` added to `best_for`.

> Self note 2026-05-17 (usage model): **this is the photographer's designated PRIMARY recipe** — the daily driver. Every other recipe is specialty or support, deployed around this one, not a co-equal alternative. The decision is always "is there a reason to step off SCU," not "which of seven." Also the **vacation/travel default** — the reliable do-anything color recipe for unknown conditions ([[synthesis/photographer-profile]]).
