# Astia/Soft

Fujifilm film simulation. Soft-contrast, gentle color rendering with restrained saturation and flattering skin tones — historically Fuji's portrait/fashion stock. On the [[entities/x100vi]] it serves as a low-contrast color base that takes well to added Color and Color Chrome without becoming harsh.
