# Mehdi Berrada

Fujifilm recipe creator, published via film.recipes. Known for strongly stylized, high-contrast color looks. Recipes here were originally developed on the X100V (X-Trans 4); they transfer to the [[entities/x100vi]] but may need verification on X-Trans 5.

## Recipes by this author

- [[recipes/loki]] — high-contrast "inky" Classic Negative, harsh-light look. https://film.recipes/2023/05/29/loki-by-mehdi-berrada/
- [[recipes/junichiro]] — dark, faded, desaturated PRO Neg. Hi, moody low-key look. https://film.recipes/2023/05/29/junichiro-by-mehdi-berrada/

Signature: deep-toned, EV −2/3 underexposed, strongly stylized. Loki = high-contrast/graphic; Junichiro = soft/grainy/desaturated.

> Self note 2026-05-17 ([[synthesis/debriefs]]): **Loki verified to transfer cleanly to X-Trans 5** (X100VI) — look intact, not broken on the newer sensor. One data point that this author's recipes carry over; Junichiro still unverified but the X-Trans 5 concern is now low-priority.
