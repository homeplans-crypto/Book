# Velvia/Vivid

Fujifilm film simulation based on Velvia slide film: high contrast and the most saturated of Fuji's color sims, with deep blues and vivid greens/reds. The landscape/nature stock; unflattering for skin. On the [[entities/x100vi]] it amplifies any added Color or Color Chrome quickly — easy to push past natural.
