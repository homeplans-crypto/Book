# Eterna / Cinema

Fujifilm film simulation modeled on Eterna motion-picture film — **low saturation, soft/flat contrast, restrained highlights and shadows**, a deliberately cinematic, understated rendering. Designed as a gradeable, undramatic base; recipes built on it often *add back* color/structure (it tolerates large Color/Clarity pushes without going garish, because the base is so flat).

## Why it matters here

Base sim of [[recipes/curtain-call]] (CUSTOM 3 — the gray/overcast/rain quiet-street tool). Relevant to the [[synthesis/photographer-profile]]: Eterna's intrinsic restraint **tames the orange-heavy local environment** (muted, not amplified) and suits a somber/rainy mood — a taste-aligned way to work flat light that is *neither* Velvia saturation ([[recipes/bay-area-fortia]]) nor Classic Chrome cool-documentary ([[recipes/stillwater-street]]). See [[concepts/saturation-in-flat-light]].
