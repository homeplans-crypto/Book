# PRO Neg. Hi

Fujifilm film simulation based on professional portrait negative film, "Hi" variant — higher contrast and a touch more color punch than PRO Neg. Std, with smooth tonal transitions and gentle skin rendering. Studio/portrait heritage; on the [[entities/x100vi]] it takes well to heavy desaturation for muted, filmic looks.
