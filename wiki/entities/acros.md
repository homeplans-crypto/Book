# ACROS

Fujifilm's premium monochrome film simulation, modeled on Neopan ACROS film: rich blacks, smooth gradation, and a refined, organic grain structure finer than the basic Monochrome sim. Available with yellow (+Y), red (+R), or green (+G) filter variants that alter contrast as the optical filters would — +R darkens skies and boosts drama. No Color control; tone is shaped by the curve, Clarity, and Monochromatic Color. The top choice for serious B&W on the [[entities/x100vi]].
