# Alan Schaller

London-based street photographer (b. 1988, London; came to photography in 2015 from music/film). Shoots **exclusively black-and-white**; co-founder of Street Photography International. [[recipes/schaller]] is a tribute to his look — not authored by him.

## Style

- **High contrast, exclusively monochrome** — deep blacks, bright whites, a deliberately timeless quality.
- **Light-led** — uses hard natural light (harsh sun, neon) to build bold, graphic shadows; light and shadow are the subject as much as the person.
- **Minimalist composition** — generous negative space, the human often small and "swallowed" by surroundings; strong geometric shapes guiding the eye.
- **Gear** — Leica M10 Monochrom, **35mm f/2** prime, fast shutter (~1/1000s) to freeze motion; edits in Lightroom / Silver Efex Pro for contrast.

## Why it matters here

His core gear is a 35mm-equiv f/2 prime — **the [[entities/x100vi]] is the same focal length and aperture**, a fixed-lens analog to his setup. The skill transfer is the point: emulating Schaller is less about a recipe and more about *seeing* hard light, negative space, and geometry. [[recipes/schaller]] (ACROS+R, Shadow +4, EV −2/3, shot in harsh daytime light) is the JPEG approximation; the technique is the real lever.

His own tips: shoot B&W *on purpose*; adjust to the light rather than fight it; use the environment; aim for a full contrast range; change perspective; edit wisely.

> Self note 2026-05-17 (two modes — **both adopted**, [Ann Demeulemeester](https://www.alanschaller.com/ann-demeulemeester)): Schaller's B&W is **not one look**. (a) **Graphic street/architecture mode** — hard light, crushed inky blacks, negative space, the scene (the Soundstage frame; what current [[recipes/schaller]], Shadow +4, dials). (b) **Dimensional/sculptural mode** (Ann Demeulemeester) — **deep but luminous, retained shadow detail, protected highlights, full tonal separation, strong-without-harsh, clean**; light revealing form. The human wants **both, as two separate recipes**: *Schaller Dark* (= mode a, kept, only de-grained + filter-retuned) and *Schaller Luminous* (= mode b, new). Mode (b) converges tonally with [[entities/helene-binet|Binet]] and absorbs her register (Binet Form recipe dropped). See [[synthesis/recipe-roster-review]].

> Source note 2026-05-17 ([[sources/schaller-night-shooting]]): a growing distillation of Schaller's own technique now lives in **[[synthesis/schaller-technique]]** (living repository, populated from his videos). Key confirmation: his night method is **EV ≈ −2, "underexpose, recover from the shadows"** — *"most of the dynamic range comes from boosting out of the shadows rather than cutting back on highlight."* This is the author's own validation of [[recipes/schaller|Schaller Dark]]'s baked-in underexpose-for-highlights philosophy (and the 2026-05-17 camera correction). Caveat: his −2 is RAW + post-boost on a Monochrom; the X100VI recipe is −2/3 baked into a JPEG — same principle, don't chase his number literally.
