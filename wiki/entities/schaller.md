# Alan Schaller

London-based street photographer (b. 1988, London; came to photography in 2015 from music/film). Shoots **exclusively black-and-white**; co-founder of Street Photography International. [[recipes/schaller]] is a tribute to his look — not authored by him.

## Style

- **High contrast, exclusively monochrome** — deep blacks, bright whites, a deliberately timeless quality.
- **Light-led** — uses hard natural light (harsh sun, neon) to build bold, graphic shadows; light and shadow are the subject as much as the person.
- **Minimalist composition** — generous negative space, the human often small and "swallowed" by surroundings; strong geometric shapes guiding the eye.
- **Gear** — Leica M10 Monochrom, **35mm f/2** prime, fast shutter (~1/1000s) to freeze motion; edits in Lightroom / Silver Efex Pro for contrast.

## Why it matters here

His core gear is a 35mm-equiv f/2 prime — **the [[entities/x100vi]] is the same focal length and aperture**, a fixed-lens analog to his setup. The skill transfer is the point: emulating Schaller is less about a recipe and more about *seeing* hard light, negative space, and geometry. [[recipes/schaller]] (ACROS+R, Shadow +4, EV −2/3, shot in harsh daytime light) is the JPEG approximation; the technique is the real lever.

His own tips: shoot B&W *on purpose*; adjust to the light rather than fight it; use the environment; aim for a full contrast range; change perspective; edit wisely.
