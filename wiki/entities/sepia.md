# Sepia

Fujifilm film simulation: fixed warm-toned monochrome (no Color control; the Monochromatic Color warm/cool slider is the only tone adjustment, separate from the toning baked into Sepia). Contrast and texture are driven entirely by the tone curve, Clarity, and Sharpness. On the [[entities/x100vi]] it suits graphic, high-contrast, texture-led work.

## Recipes using this sim

- [[recipes/brians-sepia]] — hard, gritty, high-contrast toned look (Highlight +2 / Shadow +3, Clarity +3, Sharpness +3).
