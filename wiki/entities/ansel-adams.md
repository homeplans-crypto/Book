# Ansel Adams

American landscape photographer (1902–1984), Yosemite; with Fred Archer devised the **Zone System** — a method of mapping a scene's tones to a full black→white scale (placed at capture, controlled in development/print). The canonical reference for *full tonal range* in B&W.

## Why it matters here

Named by [[entities/schaller|Alan Schaller]] in two videos: a master of geometric/leading-line composition from nature ([[sources/schaller-composition]]), and the Zone System as the model for tonality — *every tone represented, black to white* ([[sources/schaller-bw-street-tips]]).

For this wiki the Zone-System ideal is **[[recipes/schaller-luminous|Schaller Luminous]]'s explicit philosophy** (deep *but* luminous, retained shadow detail, full tonal separation). It also clarifies the two-mode split: [[recipes/schaller|Schaller Dark]]'s crushed blacks are a *deliberate exception* to "every tone represented," not Schaller's default — his default values the full range. Practice notes live in [[synthesis/schaller-technique]].
