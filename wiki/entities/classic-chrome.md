# Classic Chrome

Fujifilm film simulation — muted, understated color with a slightly flat, documentary contrast curve (modeled on a classic slide/magazine-print look). Lower saturation than Provia/Astia, deeper-feeling tones, restrained reds; favored for street, reportage, and travel where a sober palette beats pop.

## Why it matters here

The understated film sim the [[synthesis/photographer-profile]] practice wanted (quiet city streets + travel) and the one previously **unrepresented** on the camera. Used by [[recipes/stillwater-street]] — the taste-aligned replacement for [[recipes/bay-area-fortia]]'s gray-rescue role: it lifts flat/gray light with contrast and character rather than the raw saturation that over-eggs the orange-heavy local environment.
