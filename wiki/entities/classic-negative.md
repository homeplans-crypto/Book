# Classic Negative

Fujifilm film simulation modeled on consumer color negative film (Superia lineage). Distinctive tonal color shifts — slightly cyan/teal shadows, warm-but-restrained midtones — with punchy contrast and muted-yet-characterful saturation. A street/documentary favorite on the [[entities/x100vi]]; responds strongly to Color Chrome and tone-curve adjustments.

## Recipes using this sim

- [[recipes/pastel-vibes]] — soft, cooled, muted-pastel street/everyday look (Highlight −2, Color Chrome Weak, WB R−1).
