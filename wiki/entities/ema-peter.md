# Ema Peter

Architectural photographer (Vancouver-based per general industry knowledge; her [architecture portfolio](https://www.emapeter.com/architecture) itself carries no biographical text). Working at the top of the commercial-architectural field — her portfolio lists ~48 projects for major architects including **Peter Zumthor / SOM (LACMA), Tadao Ando (Bourse de Commerce), BIG, Kengo Kuma, Heatherwick, Oppenheim, KPMB, Populous**.

## Style (per [the portfolio](https://www.emapeter.com/architecture))

- **Primarily color**, often **panoramic** compositions — wide format.
- **Natural light, clean architectural lines, spatial clarity** — geometric precision and form as primary subjects.
- **Material-aware** — wood, concrete, glass rendered with attention to surface and texture.
- Tonal register **descriptive, neutral, legible** — service of the architecture rather than artistic-interpretive (a distinct register from [[entities/helene-binet|Binet]]'s atmospheric/poetic B&W).

## Why it matters here

Fills a specific slot in the [[synthesis/photographer-profile|photographer's]] admired-architectural-influence set: she is the **clean, color, legibility-first** architectural register — distinct from [[entities/schaller|Schaller]] (graphic B&W *street*), [[entities/helene-binet|Binet]] (atmospheric B&W large-format, the fragment), and [[entities/mehdi-berrada|Berrada]] (recipe author + architecture).

Cross-reference: **Peter and Binet have both photographed Zumthor** — two of the photographer's admired influences shooting the same architect, in opposite modes (Peter color/descriptive, Binet B&W/atmospheric). A quiet validation that "architectural photographer" spans more than one register inside this profile.

**Quiet observation (no recommendation):** Peter's register is *color* architectural — clean, panoramic, material-aware. The current 7-slot roster does **not** carry a dedicated architectural-color tool (the proposed *Binet Form* was dropped in favour of a second Schaller B&W mode, [[recipes/schaller-luminous]] absorbing Binet's tonal register). Peter's influence sits in *exactly* that absent register. Recorded as observation, not a slot proposal — the roster is committed/stable and the standing conclusion is that composition/seeing is the lever, not recipe coverage.
