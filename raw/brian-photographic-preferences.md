# Brian — Photographic Preferences Reference

Context file for AI assistants working on photography-related tasks (recipes, tools, writing, curation).

## Identity & Subjects

Brian is a landscape, flower, and architectural photographer shooting across all times of day and night. Primary camera: **Fujifilm X100VI**, used exclusively as SOOC (straight-out-of-camera) with custom film simulation recipes. Core philosophy: **the X100VI is an art instrument, not a documentation tool** — the phone handles plain capture. Every recipe slot is a deliberate artistic voice, not gap-filling coverage.

## Aesthetic

In his own words: **quiet, grounded, textured, found**. The fine-art vocabulary: **tonalism** (narrow muted tonal range, atmosphere over contrast), **wabi-sabi** (weathered, imperfect, transient beauty — the Tanizaki "In Praise of Shadows" thread), **cinematic** (restrained desaturated color, deep shadows, frames that feel like film stills), and **chiaroscuro/low-key** for the dark-dominant work. Subjects should feel *found* rather than dramatized.

Personal root: an Illinois upbringing among cornfields, old barns, and soil. Weathered materials, rural edges, faded paint, stone, brick, and distance are home territory.

## Lineage

Kodachrome-era documentary tradition: **Stephen Shore, William Eggleston, Joel Sternfeld**. Plus **Saul Leiter** (muted color, atmosphere), **Todd Hido** (night, suburban mood), **Josef Sudek** (tonalist still life). Contemporary recipe influence: **Mehdi Berrada** (Moroccan architect-photographer) — light on a surface, shadow as positive form, texture as presence.

## Current Seven-Slot Recipe Bank (X100VI, camera order)

1. **C1 Found Tonalism** — Classic Chrome; wide rural landscape; grounds the field while telling the truth about the subject
2. **C2 Close Perivolos** — Classic Negative (H0 S+4); intimate material still life; crushes into deep wine-red shadow
3. **C3 Mid Arch Perivolos** — Classic Negative (H−1 S−1); mid-distance flat-light architecture and street (~25–30% of shooting); earths color toward rust-orange
4. **C4 Honō** — Classic Negative; dramatic skies and weather; imposes a warm cinematic world
5. **C5 Pastel Bloom** — Astia/Soft; delicate flora in daylight; honest, high-key renderer; the one deliberate NR −2 exception
6. **C6 Loki** — Classic Negative, inherited unchanged from Berrada; the deep dramatic low-key anchor; night/shade flowers; lush luminous richness at EV 0 in contained light
7. **C7 Raku** — Acros+Ye, softened; abstracts to grey form

Note: a separate lighting-problem-organized bank variant exists (First Light, Big Sky, Pastel Bloom, Distance, Astia Field, After Dark, Loki). Ask which bank is current if it matters.

## Technical Invariants & Principles

- **NR −4 is the universal constant** (Pastel Bloom's NR −2 is the sole deliberate exception)
- **Day/night curve principle:** lifted-curve recipes (Pastel Bloom, Found Tonalism) work in bright daylight; crushed-curve recipes (Loki, Close Perivolos) need contained or low light
- Deep fluency with Fujifilm sensor mechanics: DR modes as underexpose-and-lift, tone curve interactions, WB mode trade-offs (Auto White Priority vs. fixed Kelvin vs. Daylight for light-source separation at night)
- Night street work: Reala Ace base, Daylight WB for light-source separation, DR400
- Events/people: PRO Neg. Std for variable lighting
